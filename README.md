## ✨ What you get

- **🛡️ Unlock any Medium story** — paste a link or share one from any app and read the whole article.
- **📚 Your Library** — continue reading, favorites, saved articles, and history, all in one place.
- **💾 Read offline** — save any article and open it later without a connection.
- **🔤 Make it yours** — five reader themes (Light, Dark, Sepia, OLED, System) plus font, size, and layout controls.
- **🔎 Find in article** — search inside a story and jump between matches.
- **🔄 Always loads** — automatic mirror fallback keeps articles coming when one source is down.
- **🎨 Beautiful & fast** — clean Material 3 design with dark mode.
- **🔐 Truly private** — no account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your device.
